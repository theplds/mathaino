# Μαθαίνω v0.6.82 — Real manufacturer badge identification

The **«Μόνο σήματα»** test uses seven actual manufacturer emblems
(not generated lookalikes) and does not show the entire car. Logos and
badges are normalized to the same neutral 720×480 PNG presentation.
Photo-mode remains untouched. Logos are shown for factual brand identification
in a non-commercial educational context, **not** as sponsorship or endorsement.

Images are bundled under `assets/badges/` and service-worker precached. Two source images (Porsche and Chevrolet) came from the public VehicleSpecs logo collection. Its curation is MIT-licensed, but that does **not** license manufacturer artwork; those marks remain trademarks used here only for identification.
All seven images have their original page and licence below. Public-domain
(simple geometric/text) logos can remain protected as **trademarks**, so
they must not be used to suggest an affiliation. Copyrighted badge photographs
and derivatives require attribution and acknowledgement of adaptations.
The neutral-format rasterization / compositing is a presentation adaptation
of the referenced Commons badge material. All file pages remain linked after
correct answers, and all sources are recorded permanently here.

**Learning design:** four brand choices per question; no car or model photograph;
no text labels on the image card; wrong answers allow retry but score is counted
only when the first answer is correct; seven-round summary and restart;
no timer, streak, login changes, tracking, or extra account storage.

**Important:** Many emblems contain the manufacturer name as part of the
actual logo (Ford, Nissan, Porsche, Lamborghini). This is intentional in
the starter badge test: it teaches recognition of real branding. A later
silhouette-only test could cover lettering, but must not misrepresent
an edited logo as a manufacturer's original.
