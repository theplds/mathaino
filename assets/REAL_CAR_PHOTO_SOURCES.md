# Μαθαίνω v0.6.81 — audited real car photos

## Selection rule

The ideal educational photo displays **the whole real car**, in a similar,
uncluttered setting, preferably a **front three-quarter** view. No black or
white body colors; recognizable front badges; no rear views, open bonnets,
cropped bumpers, or indoor showroom photos. These seven verified cars are
photographed **outdoors**, with standardized neutral 16:9 presentation.
The individual real-world backgrounds still vary; **these are not a fully
matched official press-photo collection**. The child may enlarge the real badge
without seeing a synthetic logo.

## Source and usage rights

Manufacturer official press files were researched but withheld because many
publishers restrict reuse to journalistic/editorial purposes unless explicit
permission is granted. The current photos come from **independent photographers
whose image pages permit reuse with attribution**. Seven JPEGs are now bundled
under `assets/cars/` and **pre-cached for offline use**, eliminating Wikimedia
image rate limits during normal play. All seven files were actually downloaded,
checked as JPEGs, and visually reviewed; the Lamborghini has been cropped to
exclude a dealership sign while preserving the entire car. That crop is an
adaptation: attribution and share-alike terms still apply.

## Quality control

Each locally bundled file has an author and source, was visually inspected for
front-oriented full-car framing, and is checked for valid JPEG bytes by CI.
A separate browser/child device test is still required to confirm badge
legibility on small screens and the PWA's offline installation behavior.
