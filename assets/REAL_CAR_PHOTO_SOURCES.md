# Μαθαίνω — real colorful front-view car photos (v0.6.80)

This quiz uses photographs of **real production automobiles**, not generated images.
Color and front/front-three-quarter framing were chosen from the source descriptions
to prioritize visible factory badges. The main photo can be magnified using
**«Μεγέθυνση σήματος»**, helping children inspect the real emblem without displaying
the brand name before the answer. Some logos may remain small or obscured in real
photographs; **visual badge legibility on a child's device has not been independently
verified for all ten images**. The child should never be asked a question if the
photo fails to load.

Photographs still stream from Wikimedia Commons. Filenames below are **planned
local JPEG filenames**, not files already bundled offline. Actual image caching
and offline reliability require a future binary-assets release, with sources,
attribution and licences retained.
