# Μαθαίνω — car brand recognition, curated v0.6.81

## Image standard

The ideal car lesson uses **full-car, front-three-quarter, brightly colored images
with simple neutral backgrounds, comparable scale, camera height and lighting**.
The photo's actual grille emblem should be recognizable at child-tablet size.
No black/white cars, interiors, rear-only shots, or artificial drawings.
The image is displayed with `object-fit: contain` in one neutral 16:9 matte;
only deliberate "Μεγέθυνση σήματος" mode magnifies it.

## Manufacturer press photography — licensing finding

Manufacturer press galleries supply excellent visual references, but **press-only
does not mean free to redistribute in an educational web app**:

- Porsche newsroom terms: https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/images-videos/porsche-newsroom-terms-and-conditions.html — journalist/media reporting only, prior agreement otherwise.
- Tesla media gallery: https://www.tesla.com/tesla-gallery — news media only; other uses require written permission.
- Ford media agreement: https://media.ford.com/ — editorial only; other uses need approval.
- Nissan newsroom policy: https://global.nissannews.com/en/pages/terms-of-use — editorial use only; written permission for other uses.

**No official press images were downloaded, hotlinked or claimed licensed.**
Instead the pilot uses reuse-permitted independent photography with source links.
The eight selected photographs are an interim *press-style goal*, not an
attested uniform press-photo set. Visual review of full-car framing, visible
emblems and background consistency remains outstanding before final signoff.

## Offline and credits

Images are currently fetched from Wikimedia Commons and are **not files already
bundled offline**. Keep the photographer's name, Commons source and license
beside the question; retaining attribution is a requirement, including for
many CC BY-SA images. A future locally hosted image pack should maintain
those notices and attribute any modifications.
